      **We can’t thank our sponsors enough; their generosity makes Makevention possible!**

      Makevention sponsors support a **maker-based learning community** that encourages and teaches everyone—from school-aged children to retired adults—to explore the creation of things and the science and engineering behind that creation. This is a unique opportunity to engage the Bloomington maker community at-large.

      Makevention provides an exhibit area where individuals, businesses, and organizations can showcase their products and services and network with attendees and other exhibitors. Makevention sponsors cover event costs that enable exhibitor fees to remain low and attendance free for the community!
