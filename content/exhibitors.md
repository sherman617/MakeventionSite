      Makevention provides businesses and local organizations in the greater Bloomington community an opportunity to provide name recognition and share their work to our growing community of motivated and creative people.

      The primary goal of Makevention is to showcase the maker individuals, clubs, businesses, and organizations in the greater Bloomington area and inspire others to get creative and inventive! Additionally, highlighting pathways to artistic and STEM careers while providing visibility to new hobbies and skills that are available in the community. We request exhibitors orient their booths to a wide range of ages and interests.

      We invite makers from educators, independent crafters and artists, community organizations, and members of our local professional STEM community to submit an Exhibitor Application to join Makevention!

      ***The Exhibitor Application Portal will open February 2027.***

      - General Booths – $10
      - Vendor Booths – $25
      - Sponsors – Booth included in sponsorship!

      Fee includes one table. Extra tables are available for $10 each and available if space permits.

      **Exhibitor fees are paid immediately after registration with PayPal or a credit card.**
