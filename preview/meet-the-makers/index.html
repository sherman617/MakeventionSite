Bloominglabs

Makevention Host Organization: Bloominglabs

Bloominglabs is Indiana’s third hackerspace (also known as a makerspace)!

Bloominglabs hosted the first Makevention in 2013 to bring together amazing and creative groups operating in Bloomington and its surrounding areas.

Bloominglabs is a community of makers, hackers, and DIY-ers who maintain a shared workshop, share skills, and collaborate. The space itself allows us to pursue projects we might not have the space and equipment for at home, and the members help keep it up. We also run various workshops and events to help one another and the wider community learn new skills.

Stop by the Bloominglabs booth at Makevention to see the talents and skills of their members and learn about becoming a member!

Public open hours every Wednesday 7:00-10:00pm.

All ages are welcome! Contact Bloominglabs: contact@bloominglabs.org.

Ways to get involved at Bloominglabs:

  • Attend a Public Open Night to learn more and experience the makerspace and meet with members;
  • Attend low-cost workshops on all kinds of cool creative topics;
  • Become a Bloominglabs member! For a monthly membership fee membership includes 24/7 access to Bloominglabs to build and make at the space, use of shared tools, and the opportunity to collaborate with the other makers in the Bloomington area!
